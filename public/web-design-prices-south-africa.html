What the monthly price buys is everything after launch. Somebody keeps the site hosted, secure and backed up. Somebody makes the changes when your prices go up or you add a service. From Standard up, somebody does the SEO every month and tells you what they did. On a once-off build those are either in the quote, billed separately, or left to you. And with us, after 12 paid months the website files are yours, so you're never stuck.

If you want to own the files on day one and you're happy to look after the site yourself, a once-off build can be the better deal. If you'd rather not think about the website at all, that's what a package is for.

What pushes a once-off quote up

Who writes the words. A designer who drops your existing text into a layout is doing a different job from one who writes each page around what your customers search for. Ask whether copywriting is in the quote. On our packages, we write the words.

How many pages, and how different they are. Five pages that share one layout cost less than five pages that each need their own design. A services page, a prices page and a contact page are three different problems.

What the site has to connect to. A payment gateway like PayFast or Yoco, a booking calendar, a CRM, a stock feed. Each one is real development time.

Tracking. Recording calls, WhatsApp taps and form enquiries is cheap to set up while the site is being built and fiddly to add later. Without it you can't tell whether the site brings in work.

Life after launch. Hosting, domain renewal, SSL, backups and edits. Ask for each as a line item, so a low build price doesn't hide a monthly fee you only see later.

What a website costs each month

Whichever way you pay for the build, a live website has running costs: hosting, the domain renewal, email, security updates and the occasional change. If you want to be found on Google, add SEO. Some quotes leave these out, some charge for them separately, and some, like ours, put them in one monthly price. Our packages cover hosting, mailboxes and monthly changes on every package, and SEO from Standard at R990 a month.

  5. What does a small change cost once it's live?
