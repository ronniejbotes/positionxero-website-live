About Position Xero: web developers in Centurion

Position Xero is a founder-led web development and SEO company based in Centurion, Gauteng. We build websites for South African businesses, coded from scratch, and look after them on monthly packages: the build is free, then from R490 a month.

Our story

Why Position Xero exists

We start where your enquiries start: a website that turns visitors into enquiries, and SEO that puts it in front of people already searching. That's the work our founders come from. Ronnie's background is web development and SEO, and Keegan's is full-stack development.

We started Position Xero after seeing the same story play out over and over: a business owner pays an agency every month, gets a pretty report full of impressions and clicks, and still has nothing to show for it.

So we made the offer simple. We build your website free, coded from scratch, and put it live within 7 days of getting your details. Then you pay one monthly price, from R490 a month, for hosting, care and changes, with SEO from the Standard package up. The four packages and everything in them are on our packages page.

Where we're based, and who we work with. Position Xero is based in Centurion, Gauteng, and we work with businesses across South Africa online, on WhatsApp, phone and video calls. If you're nearby, see web design in Pretoria and Centurion; for the rest of the country, see web developers in South Africa.
