## Monthly website packages (ZAR)
Built free: there is no build fee. You pay the monthly package from the first month.
We build the website free, coded from scratch, then host it and look after it. Package prices are monthly, in rand. No contract.
Each package includes everything in the one before it. Pay yearly: pay for 10 months, get 12.
Domain not included in any package: we can register your domain for you at cost.
- Minimum: R490/month (R4,900 a year). A website of up to 5 pages, built from our designs and tailored to your business; we write the words; hosting, security updates, backups and SSL; up to 30 minutes of small changes a month; 1 email mailbox plus free email aliases (e.g. info@ and sales@ into the same inbox); WhatsApp button and contact form; Google Business Profile set up; live within 7 days.
- Standard (recommended): R990/month (R9,900 a year). Everything in Minimum plus on-site SEO; 2 blog posts a month; Google Business Profile managed (a weekly post and review replies); Google Search Console and Bing set up; 2 mailboxes; up to 1 hour of changes a month; a monthly report in plain English.
