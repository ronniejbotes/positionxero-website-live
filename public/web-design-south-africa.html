What the best web developers do, and how to check us against it

You'll find plenty of lists of the best web developers in South Africa. A list can't tell you whether a developer suits your business. These six checks can, and they work on anyone you're talking to, us included.

  1. They show you live sites. Screenshots in a slide deck prove nothing. Ask for links you can open on your own phone. Ours are on our work page.
  2. They tell you the price before you start. Ours is published: four packages from R490 to R4,990 a month, with the website built free. See what each one includes.
  3. They give you a launch date and stand behind it. We go live within 7 days of getting your details, or your first month is free.
  4. Your domain, email and words belong to you. With us they're yours from day one, and the website files are yours after 12 paid months.
  5. They build for the phone first. 77.39% of web traffic in South Africa came from mobile devices in October 2026. Open any site they show you on your phone before anything else.
  6. They don't promise page one. Google's own guide to hiring an SEO says no one can guarantee a number one ranking. Anyone who does is guessing with your money.

And read reviews you can check, on Google or on a directory, rather than the ones a developer chose for its own homepage.

Why we build in code

There's no template or drag-and-drop builder under our package sites. We code each one from scratch, so the page carries only what it needs. That keeps it light on a phone and easy for Google to read, and it means there's no stack of plugins waiting for an update. Your phone number and a WhatsApp button sit within reach on every screen.

Because we host and look after the site, the updates are our job, and so are the small changes: new prices, new photos, a notice for the December closing. If you're deciding between a coded site and WordPress, we've laid out the trade-offs in coded website vs WordPress.
