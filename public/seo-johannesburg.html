Why the same competitors keep showing up first

Google ranks local results mainly on relevance (how well a business matches the search), distance (how far it is from the person searching) and prominence (how well known it is, which Google says draws on things like links and reviews). In a city the size of Johannesburg, you can't move closer to every customer. What you can change is how well Google can match you to the search, and how much there is to show Google that people use and rate you.

When a Johannesburg business doesn't show up, it's usually one of these:

  • Your pages say "Johannesburg" and nothing smaller. People search for their part of the city, as the suggestions below show. A page that never names your part of the city, like Sandton or Johannesburg South, gives Google less to go on for those searches.
  • Your Google Business Profile has gaps. Google says businesses with complete and accurate information are more likely to show up in local results. In practice that means your hours, categories, services, service areas and photos.
  • Your reviews stopped. Google says more reviews and positive ratings can help your local ranking.
  • Nobody can tell when you're open. If you take emergency call-outs and neither your site nor your profile says so, someone searching late at night has nothing to go on.

What people in Johannesburg and Sandton type into Google

Google says its autocomplete predictions reflect real searches done on Google. On 14 November 2026 we checked the suggestions for a few trades, and for dentists, in Johannesburg and Sandton, with the region set to South Africa.

The other searches told the same story in their own way:

  • Part of the city. Johannesburg South came up for electricians, locksmiths and pest control too, and the CBD for locksmiths and pest control. People also shorten the city: Google suggested "plumber jhb south".
  • Sandton as a place of its own. For Sandton, Google suggested "emergency" and "24 hour" for plumbers and electricians, "reviews" for plumbers and "best" for electricians.
  • The building. For dentists in Sandton, the suggestions named where they are: Sandton City, Medical Mews and Mediclinic.
  • Right now. "Near me" came up for locksmiths and pest control in Johannesburg, and "open now" for locksmiths.

What to do about it:

  1. Name the parts of Johannesburg you really serve on the page for each service. If you go out to customers, set the same areas in your Business Profile: Google allows up to 20 service areas, within about 2 hours' drive of where you're based.
  2. If customers come to you, name your building the way they would, on your contact page as well as in your profile.
  3. Say when you work. If you really answer after hours, put it on the page and put your real hours in your profile. If you don't, leave it off.
  4. Ask every customer for a review, the same way, after every job. Google's review rules don't allow offering anything in return, or asking only the customers you expect to be happy.
