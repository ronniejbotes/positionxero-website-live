What You Get

What the plan covers

Everything a small business website needs to be built, found and kept working, for one monthly amount.

Designed and built for you

You fill in a brief in your portal and upload your logo and photos. We design and build the site from it, and you review a private preview before anything goes live.

Hosting and security

Hosting, SSL, software and security updates, and backups. If something ever breaks, report it in the portal and we fix it. Fixes never count as one of your changes.

SEO basics, kept in order

Page titles, descriptions, headings, structured data, a sitemap and Google Search Console, set up at launch and checked as the site changes. Nobody can honestly promise you a ranking, and we do not.

Google Analytics, read for you

Analytics goes in at launch and we look at it every month. What we saw and what we did goes into your monthly report in the portal, in plain words.

Maintenance

Updates, broken links, contact forms that still deliver, pages that still load properly on a phone. The routine work that keeps a site working without you thinking about it.

Two changes every month

New text, new photos, a price update, a new section. Send each one from the portal and watch it go from received to done. Your two changes reset on the first of every month.

How It Works

From sign-up to a site that runs itself

Each step happens in your client portal, so you always know where your website is without having to ask.

01

Sign up and sign the debit order

Your details and the debit date that suits you, then a DebiCheck debit order through Netcash. You approve it in your own banking app, and your bank details never pass through our site.

02

Create your portal login

As soon as the debit order is signed you choose a password. The portal is where everything else happens.

03

Send your brief

Your business, services, logo, photos, colours, the pages you want and your domain. It saves as you go, so you can finish it over a few sittings.

04

Review the preview

We email you when the first version is up on a private link. Approve it, or list what to change in one message from the portal.

05

Go live

We connect your domain and launch. From then on we host it, secure it, maintain it and look at its numbers every month.

06

Ask for changes

Two changes a month, sent from the portal. You get an email when each one is done, and the monthly report shows what else we did.

Your Client Portal

Less admin for you, and for us

Most of what slows a website project down is messages: has the logo arrived, which photo goes where, is the preview ready, did the change get made. The portal answers those questions before anyone has to ask them.

  • Progress you can see: brief, building, review, launch, live
  • One place for your files: logo, photos and brand material, uploaded once
  • Preview approval in a click, or one message with every change
  • Changes left this month, and the status of each request
  • A monthly report of the work done on your site
  • Billing: your next debit date, past debits, and new bank details when you need them
