Local Search

SEO in Pretoria and Centurion: how Google picks who shows up

When someone nearby searches for a service, Google's own description of local results explains which businesses appear.

The three things Google weighs. Google's help page on local ranking names relevance, distance and prominence. Relevance is how well your profile matches the search. Distance is how far you are from the person searching, and when they don't say where they are, Google uses what it knows about their location. Prominence is how well known you are, which Google says draws on how many websites link to you and how many reviews you have.

Distance is fixed

A business based in Centurion is closer to someone searching from Centurion than to someone across town in Pretoria East, and nothing you do online moves your premises. So the work goes into the two factors you can change.

Relevance you can build

Every Business Profile field completed, a primary category chosen with care, and a page on your site for each service that says where you do it. Those are the parts Google can match to a search such as "geyser replacement Centurion".

Prominence you earn

Ask every customer for a review when the job is done. Google's review rules bar offering anything in return and bar asking only the customers you expect to be happy, so make the request part of how you finish each job.

If customers don't come to your premises, Google asks you to remove the address from your profile and set service areas, with the whole area within about 2 hours' drive of where you're based. More on this in our guides to local SEO for service businesses and getting your business on Google.

Location Pages

Should your site have a page for every suburb you serve?

Only where you have something real to say. Google's spam policies list pages "targeted at specific regions or cities that funnel users to one page" as doorway abuse. A Centurion page and a Pretoria East page each earn their place when they carry facts the other can't: the jobs you've done there, who covers that area, call-out terms for it, and photos from real work. If the only difference is the suburb name, leave the page out and put the effort into the service pages instead.
