Getting found when people nearby search

When someone searches for a service near them, Google weighs three things: relevance, distance and prominence. Relevance is how well your Business Profile and site match the search. Distance is how far you are from the person searching, and you can't change it. Prominence is how well known you are, which Google says draws on things like links to your site and your reviews.

The work goes into the two you can change. A Business Profile with every field filled in, a site that names each service and the areas you really cover, and reviews from every customer. Google's review rules bar offering anything in return and bar asking only the customers you expect to be happy, so make the request part of how you finish every job. If customers don't come to your premises, Google asks you to hide the address and set service areas instead.

On our Standard package at R990 a month we manage your Google Business Profile for you, with a weekly post and replies to your reviews, alongside on-site SEO and 2 blog posts a month. Growth adds a review-request kit and 10 directory listings.
