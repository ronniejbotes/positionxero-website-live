The short answer

It depends most on who builds it. On a website builder, a year on your own .co.za for a site of up to 25 pages with no shop or bookings costs from R110 (Google Sites plus a .co.za at xneelo) to R3,797 (Squarespace Basic plus a .co.za at Afrihost), before email, VAT where it's added and your own time. Procompare, a marketplace that sells enquiries to web designers, puts a once-off business website at R7,500 to R20,000 in its 2026 price guide, plus hosting and changes. Our packages put the build, hosting and changes in one monthly price, from R490 a month. Prices checked 15 November 2026.

How the calculator works

It adds up three ways of getting a business website on your own domain, using the prices in the table below. Each answer is a range: the low end uses the cheapest option we found for what you picked, the high end the dearest.

Build it yourself

The builder plan is the cheapest plan on each of four builders that does what you picked. Google Sites is free and has no shop, so it drops out when you tick the shop box; for bookings it pairs with a free Google Calendar booking page, which works on a personal Google account (some of its features need a paid subscription). On 1-grid it's Basic up to 10 pages, Standard from 11 pages or with a shop, and Premium for more than 35 pages or unlimited products. On Wix it's Light, or Core when you sell or take bookings, because Light doesn't include payments or scheduling. On Squarespace it's Basic, which allows selling with a 2% fee on store sales, plus Squarespace's separate Scheduling plan if you take bookings.

1-grid's builder includes a .co.za free for the first year. Wix's and Squarespace's yearly plans come with a free first-year domain too, but it can't be a .co.za: .co.za isn't on Wix's list for its free domain voucher, and Squarespace doesn't sell .co.za domains. So on Wix, Squarespace and Google Sites, and on 1-grid after the first year, the calculator uses R110 to R197 a year, what xneelo and Afrihost charge for a .co.za. Each mailbox costs R780 to about R1,415 a year. Your time is the hours you put in, at the hourly value you set.

A freelancer or a web agency

The build price comes from Procompare's 2026 website development price guide, which it bases on project costs reported by the pros on its marketplace and on other published cost data (Procompare sells enquiries to web designers). A business website is R7,500 to R20,000, an online shop R13,000 to R25,000 and a custom website of more than 25 pages R38,000 to R88,000. For the business and custom websites, the guide says the price doesn't include maintenance or content creation; the shop price is for setting the shop up. To that the calculator adds a year of hosting with email and your monthly changes, paid at R300 to R1,000 an hour, Procompare's range for web designers. Procompare doesn't split freelancers from agencies, and we couldn't find a South African source that does, so one range covers both.

Built for you on one of our packages

This uses our four packages exactly as on our packages page: the cheapest one that covers your mailboxes and your hours of changes, paid monthly or yearly, plus your domain at cost. An online shop, online bookings and sites of more than 5 pages are outside the packages, so they need a quote and the calculator shows no number for them. If you're comparing monthly packages from other companies, set side by side what each includes: pages, mailboxes, change time and who owns the files.

Wix and Microsoft charge in US dollars. We converted their prices at the Reserve Bank's rate for 13 November 2026, R16.85 to the dollar. Your bank's rate and fees decide what you actually pay. Amounts are rounded to the nearest rand.

What it leaves out

  • VAT where a price is shown before tax, and payment fees on sales, such as Squarespace Basic's 2% store fee and your payment provider's fees.
  • 1-grid's booking add-on after its free trial, because 1-grid doesn't show its price on the plans page.
  • Special offers. 1-grid's half-price yearly plans until 4 December 2026 would make Basic R697 for the first year.
  • A logo, photos, ads and SEO. On a builder you write the words yourself, inside your hours. A once-off quote may leave the writing out, so ask. On our packages we write them.
  • Your time when someone else builds the site: a briefing call and checking drafts, on either route.
