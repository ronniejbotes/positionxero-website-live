How We Work
Your First 90 Days

Exactly what happens after you sign, written down before you sign. No surprises, no black box, no waiting until month three to find out what you're paying for.

The Timeline

Your first 90 days, step by step

You get your first full report at Day 30. Here's everything that happens before and after it.

01

Day 0: Kickoff

A kickoff call to set your cost-per-lead target. You meet the person who will run your account, and a founder joins where they can. The same day, you get an intake questionnaire and an access checklist, so nothing stalls waiting on logins.

02

Week 1: Foundations

Account access, conversion tracking, and your strategy doc. For a website, the content checklist and page plan; for SEO, the technical audit, Business Profile and citation audit, and keyword mapping. Ad creative is built in-house. If the campaign calls for video, you supply the footage and we send you a shot list.

03

Weeks 2 to 5: Launch

Ad campaigns go live within 10 business days of receiving account access. A landing page typically takes one to two weeks from brief to launch and a full site three to five weeks; SEO fixes start shipping from week 4. Your first optimisation pass happens within 48 hours of launch, not at the end of the month.

04

Day 30: First Report

Your first full cost-per-lead report, measured against the target we set together on the kickoff call. What you spent, what it produced, and what happens next.

05

Monthly: Video Report

A 10-minute video report recorded by the person running your account, plus live dashboard access, so you never have to wait for the report to see the numbers.

06

Quarterly: Goal Reset

A goal-reset review with a one-page scorecard: what we hit, what we missed, and what the next quarter's target should be based on the data.

What You See

Open-book cost-per-lead reporting is the deal. You see what we see, whenever you want to see it.

Every monthly video report covers the same four things:

  • What you spent
  • Cost per lead vs. the target we set

  • Next month's plan
No Lock-In

You own everything

Some agencies keep the website and the data when you cancel. We think that's wrong, so here is what stays yours in writing.

Ad accounts in your name. Your Google Ads and Meta ad accounts belong to your business. We work inside them with access you can revoke at any time.

If we build your site or your landing pages, they live on hosting registered in your name, not ours.

All data and creative are yours. Tracking data, lead records, dashboards, ad creative: everything produced for your account belongs to you.

Cancel whenever you like and take the lot with you. Nothing gets held back and nothing gets held hostage.

The Review

Your 90-day review

Ninety days in, you get a straight account of how it's going, and you decide what happens next.

At Day 90 we go through the results with you, measured against the cost-per-lead target set at kickoff: what we hit, what we missed, what we changed and what we'd do next quarter. It's your first quarterly review, with a one-page scorecard, and it follows the Day 30 report and the monthly video reports, so nothing in it should come as a surprise. If you don't like where it's heading, you can stop at the end of that billing month, with no cancellation fee, and take everything with you.

The Exit

How to cancel

An agency that reports honestly doesn't need a contract to keep you. Everything we do is month-to-month, cancel anytime. Long lock-in contracts before any results are one of the red flags in our guide to spotting a lead generation scam.

To cancel, send us a simple email. It takes effect at the end of the billing month. There's no cancellation fee and no notice-period games, and you keep the ad accounts, the website, the data, and the creative.
