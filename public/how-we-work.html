How it works

  1. Send us a WhatsApp with your business name and what you do.
  2. Tell us about the business on a 15-minute call or in a voice note. Send your logo and some photos if you have them. We write the words.
  3. Check the site on your phone and tell us what to change. It goes live within 7 days of getting your details.
  4. Then we look after it, every month, on the package you chose.

What you need to send us

Not much. Tell us about your business on a 15-minute call or in a voice note. Send your logo and some photos if you have them. We write the words for you.

Every month after launch

The monthly price pays for everything after launch. Hosting, security updates, backups and SSL. Your mailboxes. Time each month for small changes, like new prices, photos or a notice on the homepage. From Standard up, it also pays for the SEO work: on-site SEO, blog posts, your Google Business Profile managed, and a report in plain English every month.

What we promise you

These promises are about our own work, so we can keep them. They are written into our terms.

  • 7 days. Live within 7 days of getting your details, or your first month is free.
  • No contract. Cancel with 30 days’ notice. Your domain, email and words are yours, and they go with you.
  • 12 months. After 12 paid months, the website files are yours too.

We work online

We're based in Centurion, Gauteng, and we work with businesses across South Africa online, on WhatsApp, phone and video calls. There's no office to visit, and you'll never need one.

You deal with the person doing the work

The person who runs your account is the person you talk to, with a founder close to the work and joining calls where they can. No middleman relaying messages between you and whoever is doing the job.
