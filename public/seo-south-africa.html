How Google picks local businesses

For searches with a local need, Google weighs relevance, distance and prominence. Relevance is how well your profile and pages match the search. Distance is how far you are from the person searching. Prominence is how well known you are, which draws on things like links to your site and your reviews.

You can't move closer to every customer. You can make sure every service and area you really cover has a page, your Business Profile is complete, and every customer is asked for a review. That's the work in the packages. If your business serves customers at their premises, your profile should show service areas and hide your street address.

SEO and AI search

To appear in Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode, a page has to be indexed and able to show in Search with a snippet, and Google says there are no extra requirements and no special optimisations beyond that. So the groundwork is the same: pages that are indexed, clear and answer the question. ChatGPT and Perplexity have their own crawlers, and we make sure they can read your site. Questions to ask any SEO company in South Africa

Ask these of us and of everyone else you're talking to.

  1. What ships this month? You should get a list of pages, posts and fixes.
  2. Which searches, and why those? A ranking for a phrase nobody searches brings nobody to your site.
  3. Who owns the accounts? Your website, domain, Business Profile and Search Console should be in your name.
  4. What's the notice period? Ask for month to month. Ours is 30 days, with no contract.
  5. What would make you tell me not to buy SEO? A good answer exists: no search demand for what you sell, or no way to handle more enquiries. If they can't name one, they're selling.
