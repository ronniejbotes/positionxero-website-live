Questions about our website packages

Your website is built free, then from R490 a month. Here is what that means, what the monthly price pays for and what stays yours.

Built free, and the monthly price

Is your website really free?

The build is free: there's no build fee. You pay the monthly package from the first month, from R490 a month on Minimum, or R4,900 if you pay for the year. The domain isn't included in any package; we register it for you at cost.

Why is there no build fee?

We build the website free and earn our money from the monthly package, from R490 a month. There's no contract: you can cancel with 30 days' notice, your domain, email and words go with you, and after 12 paid months the website files are yours too.

Why pay monthly instead of once-off?

A monthly package covers everything after launch: hosting, security updates, backups, small changes every month and, from Standard up, the SEO. The build is free and the packages start at R490 a month, so you’re not paying a lump sum before the site exists. There’s no contract, and after 12 paid months the website files are yours.

How much does a website cost per month in South Africa?

That depends on what you need after launch: hosting, email, changes and, if you want to be found on Google, SEO. Our packages cost R490 a month for the website, hosting, a mailbox and small changes, and R990 a month once SEO is included. Pay yearly and you pay for 10 months and get 12.

How does paying yearly work?

Pay for 10 months and you get 12 months of your package. That is 2 months free. Minimum is R4,900 a year, Standard R9,900, Growth R14,900 and Full House R49,900. Your package runs to the end of the year you paid for. If you cancel, we simply do not renew it. If we miss the 7-day launch on a yearly plan, we add one free month to the end of your year.

What is not included?

Your domain name is not included in any package. We can register your domain for you at cost. Ads management, online stores and fully custom or immersive websites are not in the packages either: tell us what you need and we will price it on your brief.

What stays yours

Do I own my website?

Your domain, your email and the words on your site are yours from day one. After 12 paid months, the website files are yours too.

Is there a contract?

No. You pay month to month, or pay yearly to get 2 months free. To cancel, give us 30 days’ notice.

What happens if I leave?

Give us 30 days’ notice. Your domain, email and words go with you. After 12 paid months, the website files go with you too.

Getting started

What do I need to send you?

Not much. Tell us about your business on a 15-minute call or in a voice note. Send your logo and some photos if you have them. We write the words for you.

How long does it take to build a website?

On a package, your site goes live within 7 days of us getting your details. If we miss that, your first month is free.

Can you move my existing website and email?

Yes, at no extra cost. Tell us where your website, domain and email are now, and we move them across for you.

Can I move my site from a free builder to you?

Yes. We build your new site free on whichever package you choose, from R490 a month, and moving your domain and email across costs nothing extra.

Do you only work with businesses in Gauteng?

No. We're based in Centurion and work with businesses across South Africa online, over WhatsApp, phone and video calls. You never need to come to us, and we don't need to come to you.

Do we need to meet in person?

No. We work online: a 15-minute call or a voice note to start, then WhatsApp and video calls. You check the site on your phone and it goes live within 7 days of us getting your details.

Do you use WordPress or Wix?

No. We code each package site from scratch, with no template or page builder underneath, and we host and look after it for you.

SEO and AI search

Can you promise I will be on page one of Google?

No. Nobody can promise a ranking, because Google decides who shows where. What we promise is the work: the SEO in your package, done every month, and from the Standard package up, a plain-English report that shows what we did.

Is there an SEO package without a website?

Our SEO packages include the website, because we build, host and look after it as part of the same price. If you already have a site, we rebuild it free on a package, from R990 a month with SEO, and move your domain and email across at no extra cost.

What if I need more SEO than Full House?

Full House covers SEO for one local business website. For bigger sites, several locations or national campaigns, tell us what you need and we will price the SEO on your brief.

Is AI SEO in the Minimum package?

Every new site we build gets structured data, an llms.txt file and a robots.txt that lets AI search crawlers in. The SEO work itself, including Search Console, Bing and the blog posts, starts on Standard at R990 a month.
