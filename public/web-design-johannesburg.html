What a Johannesburg website has to do

Your customers compare you with other businesses on their phones: 77.39% of web traffic in South Africa came from mobile devices in October 2026. The site has a few seconds to tell them what you do, where you work and how to reach you. So your number and a WhatsApp button sit on every screen, each service gets its own page that names the areas you really cover, and the contact form asks only for what you need to quote.

It also has to load properly on mobile data, which is why we code each site from scratch with nothing on the page it doesn't need.
