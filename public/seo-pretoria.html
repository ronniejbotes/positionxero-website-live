Why your business doesn't show up when people nearby search

For local results, Google weighs relevance, distance and prominence. Distance is how far each business is from the person searching, or from where Google thinks they are, and you can't change it. These are the usual reasons a Pretoria or Centurion business loses on relevance and prominence:

  • Your site never says where you work. People type "plumber pretoria east", as you'll see below. If your pages only say "Gauteng", or nothing at all, Google has less to match.
  • Your Google Business Profile is half filled in. Google says businesses with complete and accurate information are more likely to show up in local results. In practice that means the right category, your services, hours, service areas and real photos.
  • All your services on one page. A "Services" page that lists ten jobs gives Google one page to match against ten different searches. A page for each service gives it ten.
  • Few reviews, or none lately. Google says prominence draws on how many reviews you have, and that more reviews and positive ratings can help your local ranking.
  • Google hasn't indexed your pages. A page that isn't in Google's index can't show up at all. The URL Inspection tool in Search Console tells you whether each page is in.

How people in Pretoria and Centurion search

Start typing a trade and a town into Google and autocomplete fills in the rest. Google says its autocomplete predictions reflect real searches done on Google. We checked a few on 14 November 2026 with the region set to South Africa. In Pretoria, people add the side of town.

It wasn't only plumbers. For electricians, locksmiths, pest control and dentists, Google suggested Pretoria East, North and West as well, and at least one of Moot, Central or the CBD for each of them. For Centurion it suggested a suburb (Rooihuiskraal, for plumbers) and "around Centurion" or "Centurion area". And "prices" kept coming up: plumbers in Pretoria North, electricians in Pretoria East, pest control across the city.

What that means for your site:

  1. Name the parts of Pretoria you really work in, on the page for each service you do there, in plain words: "Geyser replacements in Pretoria East and Moot."
  2. Show prices or a price guide on your service pages if you can. People add "prices" because they want a number before they call.
  3. If you go out to customers, set the same areas in your Business Profile. Google lets you add up to 20 service areas and asks that the whole area stays within about 2 hours' drive of where you're based.
  4. Make a page for an area only when you have something true to say about it, like jobs you've done there. Google's spam policies treat pages made only to catch a town's searches as doorway pages.
