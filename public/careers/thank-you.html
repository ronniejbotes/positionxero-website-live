What Happens Now

How your application gets handled

01

It Reaches the Founders

Your answers are emailed to the founders through the same form service that handles every other form on this site. We do not pass applications to recruiters. Our privacy policy covers how form submissions are processed.

02

A Person Reads It

Every application is read by a person here. Position Xero is founder-led: Ronnie James Botes and Keegan Shane Haumann started it and still run it. There is more on both of them on the about page.

03

If It Fits, We Talk

A short video call about the work you have actually done, what we are building, and whether the hours line up on both sides. A founder joins where they can. Then a practical session on a real piece of work: an account walkthrough, a mock call, or a small build. No eight-round interview loop.

Need to add something to your application? Email hello [at] positionxero.com with the role name in the subject line.
