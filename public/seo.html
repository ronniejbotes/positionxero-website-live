What a month looks like on Standard

Say you're a plumber in Pretoria. In a month on Standard we'd publish two posts answering questions like "how much does a geyser replacement cost" and "what to do when your geyser bursts", post to your Business Profile every week and reply to each new review, keep the titles and descriptions on your pages sharp, and send you a report in plain English showing what we did and what Search Console says changed. You don't have to brief us on any of it.

What SEO costs elsewhere, and how to compare

SEO prices in South Africa vary because the work varies. Before you compare numbers, ask each provider what you'll actually get every month: how many pages or posts, which fixes, which listings, and what the report shows. A monthly fee with no list attached is paying for time you can't see.

Two things to check on any SEO quote, ours included:

  1. Who owns the accounts. Your website, domain, Business Profile and Search Console should be yours. With us, your domain, email and words are yours from day one, and the website files after 12 paid months.
  2. What they promise. Google's guide to hiring an SEO says no one can guarantee a number one ranking on Google. We promise the work in your package, every month, and the report that shows it.
