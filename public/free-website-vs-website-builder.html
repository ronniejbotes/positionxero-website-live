Wix, Google Sites or a website built for you: what does free really cost?

A free website builder costs you nothing in rand and a fair amount in hours. You pick the template, write every word, find the photos and fix whatever breaks, and the free version comes with the builder's branding and its own web address. Our websites are built free, in code, by us, and then you pay from R490 a month for hosting, care and changes. Which one suits you comes down to how much time you have and what the site has to do for your business.

What a free builder plan gives you

Wix's free plan needs no credit card. A free Wix site lives on an address like username.wixsite.com/sitename and carries Wix branding, and taking the branding off means moving to one of the Premium plans on Wix's own plans page.

Google Sites puts your site on a sites.google.com link unless you connect a domain you control, which means adding records at whoever manages your domain.

The free website Google used to make from your Business Profile is gone. Google turned those sites off in March 2024 and sent their visitors to the Business Profile until 10 June 2024, as Search Engine Journal reported from Google's notice. Your profile can still link to a website. You just need one somewhere else.

What you get with us, from R490 a month

On our Minimum package we build a website of up to 5 pages from our designs, tailored to your business, and we write the words. Hosting, security updates, backups and SSL are included. So are up to 30 minutes of small changes a month, a mailbox on your own domain with free aliases like info@ and sales@, a WhatsApp button and contact form, and your Google Business Profile set up. The site goes live within 7 days of getting your details, or your first month is free.

The build itself is free. There's no build fee and no contract: you pay R490 a month, or R4,900 for a year, and you can cancel with 30 days' notice. Your domain, email and words are yours from day one, and after 12 paid months the website files are yours too. The domain isn't in any package; we register it for you at cost.

When a free builder is the better choice

If you're testing an idea, money is tight this month or you enjoy building things yourself, a free builder is a sensible place to start. The same goes if you need something up by Saturday and you can live with the builder's address for a while. You can move later, and moving your domain and email to us costs nothing extra.

When R490 a month makes more sense

When your hours are worth more running the business than dragging boxes around a screen. When you want your own domain and an email address that matches it. And when the site has to bring in work, because then somebody has to keep it current and help it get found.

That last part starts on our Standard package at R990 a month: on-site SEO, 2 blog posts a month, your Google Business Profile managed with a weekly post and review replies, Google Search Console and Bing set up, and a monthly report in plain English. Compare all four packages before you decide.
