A free website builder costs you nothing in rand and a fair amount in hours. You pick the template, write every word, find the photos and fix whatever breaks, and the free version comes with the builder's branding and its own web address. We build your website free, in code, and then you pay from R490 a month for hosting, care and changes. Which one suits you comes down to how much time you have and what the site has to do for your business.

What a free builder plan gives you

Wix's free plan needs no credit card. A free Wix site lives on an address like username.wixsite.com/sitename and carries Wix branding, and taking the branding off means moving to one of the Premium plans on Wix's own plans page.

Google Sites puts your site on a sites.google.com link unless you connect a domain you control, which means adding records at whoever manages your domain.

What Wix, Squarespace and 1-grid cost on your own domain

Putting a builder site on your own domain, without the builder's branding, costs money on all four builders below, even if only for the domain. Here's the cheapest way to do it on each, at the prices their own pages showed a visitor in South Africa on 14 November 2026, next to our Minimum package.

  • Google Sites doesn't need a paid plan for this. On a personal Google account you connect a domain you control and pay only for the domain, wherever you register it.
  • 1-grid Website Builder Basic is R129 a month paying monthly, or R1,393 for a year at the regular price, on 1-grid's website builder page. When we checked, yearly plans were half price until 4 December 2026, which made Basic R697 for the year. A .co.za, .online or .site domain comes free for the first year.
  • Wix Light is US$17 a month on a yearly plan, paid upfront and before tax. Looking from South Africa, Wix's plans page showed us its prices in US dollars. The plan includes a domain free for the first year, though not a .co.za: it isn't on Wix's list for that voucher.
  • Squarespace Basic is R300 a month paid yearly (R3,600) or R430 paying month to month, on Squarespace's pricing page. There's no free plan: every Squarespace site starts with a 14-day free trial. Yearly plans include a domain free for the first year, but Squarespace doesn't sell .co.za domains, so a .co.za is registered elsewhere and connected.
  • Our Minimum package is R490 a month, or R4,900 for a year. We build the site free and write the words, and hosting, small changes and a mailbox are included. The domain is extra: we register it for you at cost.

Can you still get a free website from Google My Business?

No. Google Business Profile, which many people still call Google My Business, used to make a simple free website from your listing. From 5 March 2024, Google switched those websites off. Visitors were sent to the business's profile until 10 June 2024, and after that they got a “Page not found” error.

Addresses ending in business.site and negocio.site were also taken out of the website field on profiles. All of that comes from Google's help page on the shutdown. Google has since retired that page, so the link opens a saved copy of it from February 2025, kept by the Internet Archive.

The profile itself is still free. On its getting started page, read on 14 November 2026, Google says you can manage how your business shows up on Search and Maps at no charge, with your hours, phone number, location and a link to your website. A profile is only for businesses that meet their customers face to face, at a place customers can visit or by going out to them. Google says online-only businesses aren't eligible.

So if a free Google website was your plan, here's what you can do now:

  • Use the profile fully. Keep your hours and phone number current. If your type of business offers them, add booking or ordering links: Google allows up to 10 of each kind, for things like appointments, reservations and orders, on its local business links page.
  • Make a free site somewhere else and link it. Google's shutdown page listed other site builders, Google Sites among them, and said to put the new address on your profile. Google Sites needs no paid plan. It puts your site on a sites.google.com link, or on your own domain, where you pay only for the domain (see the chart above). The address goes in the Website field of your profile, as Google's add your business website page shows.
  • Have one built for you. We build your website free, in code, and you pay from R490 a month. Your Google Business Profile is in every package if your business qualifies for one: we set it up on Minimum and manage it from Standard up.

When a free builder is the better choice

If you're testing an idea, money is tight this month or you enjoy building things yourself, a free builder is a sensible place to start. The same goes if you need something up by Saturday and you can live with the builder's address for a while. You can move later, and moving your domain and email to us costs nothing extra.

Is Wix actually free?

Wix has a free plan that needs no credit card. A free Wix site sits on an address like username.wixsite.com/sitename and shows Wix branding, and removing the branding takes one of Wix's Premium plans, according to Wix's plans page (read 12 November 2026).

About R3,437 for a year, before tax, on Light, Wix's cheapest Premium plan, which connects your own domain and removes Wix branding. Wix showed us its prices in US dollars from South Africa: US$17 a month on a yearly plan, paid upfront, on Wix's plans page on 14 November 2026. That's US$204 for the year, which we converted at the Reserve Bank's rate for 13 November 2026 (R16.85 to the dollar). Your bank's rate and fees decide what you actually pay.

Does Google My Business still give you a free website?

No. Google switched off the websites made with Business Profiles from 5 March 2024, and since 10 June 2024 their old addresses show a “Page not found” error, according to Google's help page on the change (now kept by the Internet Archive). The Business Profile itself is still free for businesses that meet customers face to face, and it can link to a website you have elsewhere. We build that website free, and you pay from R490 a month.
