Questions

Questions about premium work

What does “from” mean on these prices?

It is where each option starts. Your final price depends on your brief, such as how many pages you need and what the site has to connect to. We talk it through with you first.

Why do some options say “priced on your brief”?

An immersive 3D site, an online store, lead generation and AI work all depend on what you want built, so we price them once we know your brief. We do not print a number we cannot stand behind.

Is there a contract?

No. Monthly work, such as SEO and ads, runs month to month with no lock-in contract.

Can you promise I will be on page one of Google?

No. Nobody can promise a ranking, because Google decides who shows where. What we promise is the work we agree with you, done every month, and a report that shows what we did.

I only need a simple website. Is premium right for me?

Probably not. Our monthly packages start at R490 a month with no build fee, and we look after the site for you.
