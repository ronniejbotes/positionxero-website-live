How a pay-monthly website works

You don’t pay to have the site built. We build it free, coded from scratch, and your first payment is your first month on the package: R490 on Minimum, R990 on Standard, R1,490 on Growth or R4,990 on Full House. Pay for the year instead and you pay for 10 months and get 12.

The monthly price pays for everything after launch. Hosting, security updates, backups and SSL. Your mailboxes. Time each month for small changes, like new prices, photos or a notice on the homepage. From Standard up, it also pays for the SEO work: on-site SEO, blog posts, your Google Business Profile managed, and a report in plain English every month.

There’s no contract. Cancel with 30 days’ notice and your domain, email and words go with you. After 12 paid months, the website files are yours too. If you already have a website and email somewhere else, we move them across for you at no extra cost.

Pay monthly or once-off?

A once-off quote buys the build. Procompare, a marketplace that sells enquiries to web designers, puts a business website at R7,500 to R20,000 once-off in its 2026 price guide, from costs its designers report. After that you still need hosting, someone to make changes and, if you want to be found, SEO, so ask every once-off quote what those cost and whether they’re included.

On a package they’re in the price. Minimum costs R5,880 over a year paid monthly, or R4,900 paid yearly, with hosting, changes and a mailbox included. If you’d rather own the site outright on day one and look after it yourself, a once-off build may suit you better. If you’d rather not think about the website at all, a package is built for that. What counts as a small change

Each package comes with time for small changes to your website every month. Here is what that covers.

Small changes

  • Changing words, prices, phone numbers or opening hours
  • Adding or swapping photos
  • Adding a new service or product to a page you already have
  • Putting a special or a notice on your site

Bigger jobs, quoted on their own

  • A new design or a full redesign
  • New features, such as online booking, a shop or a members’ area
  • Connecting your site to a new system, such as a CRM

Fair use

Your change time is for small changes to the website we built for you. Time you do not use does not carry over to the next month. A bigger job is quoted on its own, so you can decide before it starts.

Questions about the packages

Do I own my website?

Your domain, your email and the words on your site are yours from day one. After 12 paid months, the website files are yours too.

Is there a contract?

No. You pay month to month, or pay yearly to get 2 months free. To cancel, give us 30 days’ notice.

What happens if I leave?

Give us 30 days’ notice. Your domain, email and words go with you. After 12 paid months, the website files go with you too.

Can you promise I will be on page one of Google?

No. Nobody can promise a ranking, because Google decides who shows where. What we promise is the work: the SEO in your package, done every month, and from the Standard package up, a plain-English report that shows what we did.

What do I need to send you?

Not much. Tell us about your business on a 15-minute call or in a voice note. Send your logo and some photos if you have them. We write the words for you.

Can you move my existing website and email?

Yes, at no extra cost. Tell us where your website, domain and email are now, and we move them across for you.

What is not included?

Your domain name is not included in any package. We can register your domain for you at cost. Ads management, online stores and fully custom or immersive websites are not in the packages either: tell us what you need and we will price it on your brief.

How does paying yearly work?

Pay for 10 months and you get 12 months of your package. That is 2 months free. Minimum is R4,900 a year, Standard R9,900, Growth R14,900 and Full House R49,900. Your package runs to the end of the year you paid for. If you cancel, we simply do not renew it. If we miss the 7-day launch on a yearly plan, we add one free month to the end of your year.

What if I need more SEO than Full House?

Full House covers SEO for one local business website. For bigger sites, several locations or national campaigns, tell us what you need and we will price the SEO on your brief.

Why pay monthly instead of once-off?

A monthly package covers everything after launch: hosting, security updates, backups, small changes every month and, from Standard up, the SEO. The build is free and the packages start at R490 a month, so you’re not paying a lump sum before the site exists. There’s no contract, and after 12 paid months the website files are yours.
